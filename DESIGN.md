## Brand & Style
The design system embodies intentional vitality, balance, and modern Japanese-inspired aesthetic precision. Built for creators, lifestyle platforms, and high-energy productivity workflows, it pairs purposeful clarity with an unmistakable human warmth. The aesthetic direction fuses modern minimalism with expressive typography: pristine backgrounds establish serene breathing room, while structured silhouettes and energetic accents provide momentum and purpose.

The visual style leans into a clean, editorial warmth—avoiding clinical sterility through organic curves, dynamic headline geometry, and atmospheric depth. The interface prioritizes high legibility and instant visual hierarchy, cultivating feelings of optimism, laser focus, and seamless flow.

## Colors
The color palette relies on crisp surface contrast, grounded typography, and expressive natural pigments. 

- **Primary Accent (`#f4511e` - Sunset Orange):** Radiates energy and purpose. Reserved for primary calls-to-action, key visual focal points, interactive indicators, and active states.
- **Secondary Accent (`#2e7d32` - Emerald Green):** Represents balance, vitality, positive affirmation, and progression. Applied to secondary tags, success metrics, confirmation notifications, and natural contrasting elements.
- **Base Neutrals:** Pure white (`#ffffff`) serves as the foundational canvas, layered against a soft off-white surface tone (`#f8f9fa`) for recessed panels and grouped sections. Structural borders use soft slate-gray (`#f1f5f9` and `#e2e8f0`).
- **Typography & Content Neutrals:** Deep charcoal (`#0f172a`) anchors high-impact headings and primary labels for crisp WCAG AAA compliance, while slate (`#334155`) supports body copy and secondary metadata at strict WCAG AA standards. Subtle iconography and inactive states use muted slate (`#64748b`).

## Typography
Typography is built around the confident pairing of **Syne** and **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Syne** brings bold, avant-garde character and architectural geometry to display titles, large headers, and hero banners. It creates immediate brand distinction without sacrificing structural form.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a friendly, geometric, and remarkably legible counterbalance for all intermediate headings, body paragraphs, and UI labels. Its wide apertures and crisp terminals guarantee fatigue-free reading across varied display densities.
- Letter spacing is tightened progressively as headings increase in scale to retain visual cohesion, while UI microcopy utilizes slight positive tracking for crisp readability at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system implements a fluid 12-column grid on desktop, scaling down to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a flexible 4-column structure on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width containment of 1280px with 48px (`margin: 3rem`) outer padding and 24px (`gutter: 1.5rem`) between layout columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid grid with 32px (`margin-tablet: 2rem`) margins and 20px gutters. Multi-column cards collapse to 2-column groups.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 20px (`margin-mobile: 1.25rem`) gutters and canvas padding. Horizontal card sequences reflow to vertical stacks or native swipe-snap rails.

All spacing units adhere to an intentional 8pt grid with 4pt half-steps (`0.25rem` and `0.75rem`) dedicated to micro-alignments, chip insets, and compact button labels.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this light system relies on subtle spatial layering rather than harsh, heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:** 
  - *Tier 0 (Canvas):* `#f8f9fa` backdrop providing a quiet ambient base.
  - *Tier 1 (Cards & Modules):* Pure `#ffffff` elevated containers framed by an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #f1f5f9`).
  - *Tier 2 (Floating & Active Controls):* Modals, popovers, and elevated action buttons resting above the primary content.
- **Shadow Profiles:**
  - *Low Elevation (Resting Cards):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
  - *Mid Elevation (Hover States & Dropdowns):* `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
  - *High Elevation (Dialogs & Floating Sheets):* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Atmospheric Highlights:** Hover interactions pair slight Y-axis upward translation (-2px) with a warmer transition in the soft perimeter shadow, avoiding sterile gray diffusion.

## Shapes
The shape language features an organic, balanced curvature that balances modern software efficiency with human approachability.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Standard controls, inputs, badges, and segmented toggles.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px):** Content containers, cards, visual banners, and popover panels.
- **Extra Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** System modals, hero media wrappers, and floating action sheets.
- **Full Radius (9999px):** Pills, interactive indicator badges, and primary action buttons, ensuring a soft touchpoint for high-priority user actions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Sunset Orange (`#f4511e`) solid fill, pure white text (`#ffffff`), `font-weight: 600`, full pill rounding (`9999px`) or `rounded-lg` (16px) depending on contextual hierarchy. Hover triggers a subtle brightness lift and a warm diffused shadow (`0 6px 16px rgba(244, 81, 30, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary:** Clean white background (`#ffffff`), 1px border (`#e2e8f0`), charcoal text (`#0f172a`). Hover introduces background tint (`#f8f9fa`) and border emphasis (`#cbd5e1`).
- **Tertiary / Success Action:** Emerald green (`#2e7d32`) accent pill used for key confirmations, positive milestone tracking, and secondary completion triggers.

### Cards & Containers
- Cards feature `#ffffff` background fills set over the soft `#f8f9fa` canvas.
- Perimeter defined by a delicate `1px solid #f1f5f9` stroke combined with Low Elevation shadowing.
- Internal card padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for mobile.
- Headers inside cards utilize Syne for concise module labeling.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact pills with soft, desaturated tint backgrounds:
  - Active / Sunset: `rgba(244, 81, 30, 0.10)` background with `#f4511e` text.
  - Success / Emerald: `rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.10)` background with `#2e7d32` text.
  - Neutral / Inactive: `#f1f5f9` background with `#334155` text.
- **Filter Chips:** Interactive rounded pills (`0.5rem`) with 1px border, transitioning to solid charcoal (`#0f172a`) or primary sunset orange when activated.

### Input Fields
- Pristine `#ffffff` fill with a `1px solid #e2e8f0` border and `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius.
- Placeholder copy rendered in soft slate (`#94a3b8`).
- **Focus State:** 1px perimeter shift to `#f4511e` accented with a soft outer ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(244, 81, 30, 0.15)`). Zero harsh black browser defaults.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular and squircle controls with a default `1.5px solid #cbd5e1` outline on white.
- Selected state fills solidly with either Sunset Orange (interaction paths) or Emerald Green (checklist validations) containing a crisp white vector glyph.

### Lists & Navigation
- Interactive list items feature generous click targets (minimum 44px height) with rounded selection states (`0.5rem`).
- Hover states initiate a smooth fade to `#f1f5f9`.
- Active navigation items display an accented vertical bar indicator or sunset-tinted background pill.
